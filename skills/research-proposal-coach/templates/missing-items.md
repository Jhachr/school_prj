# Missing Items Template

```markdown
## Missing Items

| Item | Why It Matters | Owner | Suggested Deadline | Source Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
```

Common missing items:

- Literature retrieval source or results.
- Teacher data/resource details.
- Instrument review, pilot, or validation route.
- Implementation conditions or collaboration resources.

Add goal-specific items only when that goal is active:

- `project_application`: real application guide, school template, reviewer standards, or strong application examples.
- `paper`: target venue requirements, paper structure, analysis details, or reporting standards.
- `teaching_improvement`: implementation schedule, observation plan, reusable materials, or local review conditions.
