# Innovation Design Module

## Purpose

Derive realistic innovation points from the teacher's context, evidence, methods, and outcomes.

## Required Inputs

- Literature review or explicit missing evidence status.
- Research question.
- Method and outcome map.
- Local context or school/course constraints.

## Coaching Loop

1. Identify possible innovation sources: scene, object, process, tool, evaluation, outcome, or local application.
2. Compare against known literature, cases, or current school practice.
3. Draft 2-3 innovation points.
4. Explain the support behind each point.
5. Downgrade unsupported claims.
6. Ask the teacher which points feel true and defensible.

## Pass Conditions

- Each innovation point is supported by prior modules.
- No innovation point relies on invented literature or inflated language.

## Output

```markdown
### Innovation Points

| Innovation Point | Supporting Basis | Source Status | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
```

## Fallback

If evidence is missing, use modest wording such as "拟探索" and mark literature comparison as `missing`.
