# Reviewer Standards

These standards combine customer feedback from the 2026-07-15 and 2026-08-20 meetings with the current project design. Replace or refine them when school reviewers provide official standards.

## Topic Quality Pointers

A strong topic usually does several of these:

- Continues or reasonably extends the teacher's prior teaching, research, project, or course-building basis.
- Solves a real problem from teaching, administration, professional development, or school work.
- Connects to school priorities, professional cluster construction, vocational education policy, or current application guide orientation.
- Has a concrete implementation scene and available resource conditions.
- Shows an appropriate innovation angle in method, tool, process, evaluation, or local application.
- Produces reusable outcomes beyond a paper.

A topic does not need to maximize every pointer. It may emphasize one or two, but the tradeoff should be explicit.

## Common Weaknesses

- Starts from "AI + education" or "improve quality" without a concrete scene.
- Treats a task as a research problem.
- Lists methods that could apply anywhere.
- Claims innovation without comparing to literature, cases, or current practice.
- Produces only a paper and no reusable practical artifact.
- Ignores the school's guide, template, or evaluation preference.

## Outcome Expectations

Encourage at least one of:

- Task package.
- Rubric or evaluation scale.
- Process handbook.
- Case library.
- Course implementation plan.
- Data review template.
- Tool or prompt package.
- Training material.
- Practice improvement report.
